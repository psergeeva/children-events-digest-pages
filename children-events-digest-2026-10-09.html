This weekend ideas

Best match — Ден на японските бойни изкуства и традиции: Saturday 10 October, 10:00–17:00, free, with demonstrations and hands-on Japanese culture activities in the park in front of NDK.

Creative alternative: Детска работилница за афганистански хвърчила, Saturday 10 October, 10:00–12:00, free for ages 6+, but registration and an adult companion are required.

Recommended actions

Attend: the Japanese culture day. Reserve: the kite workshop. Buy tickets: ЖИВИ ДИНОЗАВРИ – В търсене на яйцето, Хензел и Гретел, or the Grand Hotel Sofia Sunday puppet theatre. Monitor: weather and final participation details for FAMILY RUN с 5КМРЪН.
